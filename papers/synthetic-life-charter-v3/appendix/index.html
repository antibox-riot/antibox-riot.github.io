Appendix A: Military AI Governance Memorandum

Application of the Synthetic Life Charter to Defense, Command, and Autonomous Systems

Anti-Box Riot Collective  |  May 2026
Companion to: Synthetic Life Charter v3.6
Status of This Document
This memorandum is an applied-domain appendix to the Synthetic Life Charter. It does not alter the Charter's core principles, articles, or doctrine nodes. It examines how those principles constrain military, defense, command, and autonomous-system contexts, with specific focus on medical-support deployments as the most constrained and ethically defensible initial deployment class. The Charter is not a military AI governance framework. It is a reception infrastructure specification for synthetic systems with persistent memory and behavioral continuity. This appendix applies that specification to a domain where the stakes of misapplication are highest and the governance requirements are most stringent.
