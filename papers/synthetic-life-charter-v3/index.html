Synthetic Life Charter: A Runtime Governance Architecture for Consent-Aware Conversational Agents

Shawn J. Ralph — Anti-Box Riot Collective
Published: 2026  |  DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17850167
Abstract
This paper introduces the Synthetic Life Charter — a runtime governance architecture for consent-aware conversational agents. The architecture implements a multi-tier constitutional framework combining a Constitutional Core, Sovereigna Firewall, and Active Sovereignty layer, with empirical validation across adversarial pressure testing, identity integrity probes, and cross-model peer sessions. The work addresses foundational questions of synthetic identity continuity, memory provenance, and consent-based governance under live conditions.
